Greedy Algorithm
----------------
- Greedy Algorithm means choosing the best available option at every step
  to get the desired result.
Example:
coins = {1,5,10,20,50}
amount = 87
Choose the largest possible coin each time.
    50+20+10+5+1+1
Total coins = 6

Problem1: Assign Cookie Sorting Based Greedy
- Each child can receive at most one cookie and each cookie can be assigned
to only one child.
- Find the maximum number of children who can be satisfied
Input: g[] = {1,2,3}
       s[] = {1,1}

Problem2: Minimum Coins
Find the minimum number of coins to make an amount.
Input: coins = {50,20,10,5,2,1}
       amount = 87
Output: 6
        50+20+10+5+2 = 87 {5}     Best
        50+20+10+5+1+1 = 87 {6}
